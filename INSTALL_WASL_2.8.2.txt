2. Extract this ZIP over your CURRENT Wasl mobile project and allow overwrite.
3. Do not delete your existing assets/fonts folder; this update does not include font binaries.

& "E:\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" clean
& "E:\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" pub get
& "E:\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" analyze
& "E:\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" test
& "E:\flutter\bin\flutter.bat" run

Expected: no analyzer errors and all tests pass.
